Your AI Learning Journey

Every path starts with the foundation

Then it branches into a No-Code track, a Developer track, or both. One structure, three ways through it.

  1. Step 01

    AI Foundation

    8–10 hours

    Core concepts, tools, and the mindset every AI-fluent professional needs. Prompting, model behaviour, and where AI genuinely fits in enterprise work.

    Outcome You can reason about AI systems and use them well in your own role.

  3. Step 02

    Agentic AI Foundation

    8–10 hours

    Agent architecture, orchestration, and multi-agent design patterns. How agents plan, call tools, hold memory, and hand work to one another.

    Outcome You can design an agentic system, not just describe one.

  4. then choose your track

  5. Stream 2

    No-Code Agents

    8 hours

    Build and ship working agents without writing a single line of code.

    • n8n
    • Voiceflow
    • RAG

    Outcome AI-powered agents and automation, without code.

    Stream 3

    Developer Track

    22 hours

    Agentic frameworks plus Claude Code, ending in a production-ready Agentic AI project.

    • Claude Code
    • MCP
    • Agent frameworks

    Outcome Production agents, sub-agents and a full Agentic project.

  7. Both Streams

    Take both tracks and cover the full spectrum — from no-code automation for the business to production level Agentic Solutions for engineering.
